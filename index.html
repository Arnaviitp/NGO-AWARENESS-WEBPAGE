About the NGO

We are a vibrant community of passionate individuals dedicated to bringing systemic change through grassroots action.

Who We Are

InAmigos Foundation is a youth-led Non-Governmental Organization committed to nurturing society through impactful campaigns and hands-on welfare projects. Our vision is a world where every individual, regardless of their background, has access to quality education, a clean environment, and equal opportunities.

Vision

A self-reliant and equitable society.

Mission

Empowering marginalized communities.

Values

Empathy, Integrity, Action.
